# Church tax opacity

Most new 501(c)(3)s apply on Form 1023 and file Form 990 every year. Churches do not have to apply: 26 U.S.C. § 508(c)(1)(A) excepts them from the notice requirement. They do not have to file a 990: § 6033(a)(3)(A)(i) excepts churches, their integrated auxiliaries, and conventions of churches. IRS examinations sit behind § 7611: a high-level official's written reasonable belief, special notices, and limits on inspecting "church records." The analog is a secular charity — or a "religious" nonprofit that is not a church — whose donors and the public can read a 990 and whose exam is an ordinary audit.

Commercial sidelines (book sales, media, real estate, related for-profits) inherit the opacity unless someone admits unrelated business income and files a 990-T. That is the modern megachurch and televangelism pattern: **for-profit activity beside a silent church**.

This page does not decide whether any ministry is sincere. It names who must show the books.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a dark franchise. The parties who obtain it are organizations that qualify as churches (and auxiliaries/conventions). Donors, members, journalists, and competing charities take the residual: no public return. The IRS takes a higher procedural bar than for other exempts.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Form 1023 + annual 990 for a food bank, university, or parachurch ministry that is not classified as a church. Private foundations have still tighter disclosure. The closed analog is a church-scale budget on the same paper as other 501(c)(3)s.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** *Bob Jones University v. United States*, 461 U.S. 574 (1983), revoked exemption for a religious school's racial policy — the rare public loss. Ordinary church 990 silence and § 7611 remain. Scientology's 1993 closing agreement ended a long exemption war and produced church status; the analog new religion still fights classification. Document the closing agreement as a routing event, not as a theology holding.

Johnson Amendment (campaign intervention ban in § 501(c)(3), 1954) theoretically binds churches and other charities alike. Enforcement against pulpits is thin relative to 990-filing entities whose political spending is visible. Structural: **no 990 means no public political-spending line**.

## Formal Legal Instruments

| Instrument | Role in the outcome | Citation |
| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- |
| Automatic exemption | Churches need not give § 508 notice to be 501(c)(3) | [26 U.S.C. § 508(c)(1)(A)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/508) |
| No Form 990 | Churches, integrated auxiliaries, conventions excepted from the information return | [26 U.S.C. § 6033(a)(3)(A)(i)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6033) |
| Church tax inquiry wall | High-official reasonable belief, notices, limits on church records | [26 U.S.C. § 7611](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/7611) |
| Johnson Amendment | Campaign-intervention ban on all 501(c)(3)s, including churches | 26 U.S.C. § 501(c)(3) (1954 overlay) |
| UBIT / 990-T | Unrelated business still theoretically taxed; only if acknowledged | 26 U.S.C. §§ 511–513 |

Corporation sole and "integrated auxiliary" are the entity forms that extend the silence. A related for-profit can sit beside the church without putting the church's core books on a 990.

## Routing

Enabled: exist as a church; skip 1023 and 990; contest any IRS contact under § 7611. Closed: public financials as a condition of exemption; ordinary audit.

## Application Evidence

The Code sections are the mechanism. IRS church tax inquiries are rare relative to 990-based exams of other exempts; GAO and TIGTA have described § 7611 as a constraint — cite those reports by year rather than inventing an inquiry count.

Scientology–IRS closing agreement (October 1993) is the large classification event: exemption recognized after litigation. Scope: that agreement, that organization. Megachurch and broadcast ministries that do not file 990s are the commercial-adjacent application. Do not invent net-asset figures that do not appear on a public return — that absence **is** the evidence.
