# Designated cost-plus and OTA contractors

Ordinary federal buying prefers full and open competition (CICA). Cost-reimbursement contracts (FAR Part 16.3) shift cost risk to the government. Other Transaction Authority lets DoD and HHS/BARDA award agreements that are **not** FAR contracts, with follow-on production that can skip a second competition. Operation Warp Speed (2020) used BARDA money, DoD contracting channels, and OT/intermediary awards to designated vaccine and manufacturing firms, then paired the product with PREP immunity ([pla-042](covered-person-liability-shields.md)). The analog is a small firm bidding a sealed, competed, firm-fixed-price job for the same kind of work.

The military-industrial complex is the home of this enabler. The health complex reused it for medical countermeasures. 1033 ([pla-040](militarized-local-enforcement.md)) is the second market: gear the MIC was paid to make becomes police property.

This page does not decide whether any countermeasure "worked." It names **who was designated to be paid**.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a legally preferred counterparty: first receiver of appropriated or emergency funds, often with cost-plus or OT terms, sometimes with a non-FAR intermediary. The parties who obtain it are the named contractors and the agencies that need a fast, legally special award. Other equipped firms take the closed analog: a competition that never opens, or a FAR protest process that OTAs were built to avoid.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

FAR Part 14 sealed bidding. A firm-fixed-price contract where the vendor eats overruns. Bayh-Dole exclusive license of a publicly funded patent ([pla-019](bayh-dole-exclusive-license.md)) is the IP cousin: public money, private residual. Fiat first-receiver issue ([pla-029](fiat-first-receiver-impulse.md)) is the monetary cousin: new purchasing power at a designated desk. The closed analog is an un-designated shop with the same machines bidding on equal paper.

**Other complex:** classic DoD cost-plus weapons programs; intelligence community cost-plus SICs; energy-complex cost-plus at national labs. Health-complex BioShield/BARDA is the same shape with a different product.

OWS awards were a mix: BARDA OTs, DoD-channel contracts, and funds routed through a defense contracting intermediary (Advanced Technologies International), which NPR reported in 2020 as putting more than $6 billion under less-transparent DoD-style vehicles. Cite that as contemporaneous reporting of **vehicle**, not as a GAO audit of every dollar.

## Routing

Enabled: OT, cost-reimbursement, sole-source/exception, intermediary. Closed: a protestable FAR competition that any equipped vendor can enter as of right.

PREP immunity on the same product is the liability half. Designation to make it is the purse half. Mandates ([pla-043](funding-conditioned-mandates.md)) are the demand half.

## Application Evidence

10 U.S.C. § 4022 and 42 U.S.C. § 247d-7e(c)(5) are the OT pipes. NPR (Sept. 29, 2020): more than $6 billion in OWS vaccine funding routed through ATI, with contemporaneous figures including about $1.95 billion (Pfizer), $1.6 billion (Novavax), $1.79 billion (Sanofi), $1 billion (J&J). Scope: that 2020 reporting, those named awards, not a total program spend. CRS IN11560 tracks OWS contract lists. Emergent BioSolutions CDMO task order (~$628 million, June 2020 HHS release) is a named manufacturing designation. Wikipedia and later recaps of an ~$10–18 billion OWS envelope are secondary; prefer CRS/HHS releases for expansion.

1033 transfers ([pla-040](militarized-local-enforcement.md)) are application evidence that MIC production does not end at the base. Surplus is a legally authorized second market.

## Framework notes

Relevant distinction rules from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md):

- **Rule 1 (Pride vs Humility and Equality).** The designated contractor is not equal to the analog bidder.
- **Rule 15 (Response to loss: retake or expand).** Cost-reimbursement expands the claim on the Treasury rather than capping the vendor's loss.
- **Rule 33 (Dominion: stewardship or ownership).** Public money becomes a private franchise to make the product.
- **Rule 38 (Consequences: natural or artificial).** OT and intermediary vehicles are man-made tracks around competition.
- **Rule 42 (Production: distributed or centralized).** Many shops could make vials or code; the award list is centralized.

Do not score this entry. The rules name the pattern; they do not replace the instruments or the evidence.
