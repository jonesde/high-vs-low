# Private foundation perpetual control

Carnegie, Rockefeller, and later Ford parked first-receiver industrial wealth in perpetual foundations that make policy grants. Congress answered abuses with the Tax Reform Act of 1969: a minimum distribution (§ 4942, generally 5% of non-charitable assets), self-dealing excises (§ 4941), jeopardy-investment and taxable-expenditure rules, an audit tax, and 990-PF disclosure. The analog complete gift to living poor is spent and gone. 1969 **regulated and thereby legalized** the vehicle: deducted capital stays under family or appointed-board control in perpetuity, so long as the 5% and the paper are kept.

Controllers: founding families, appointed boards, investment staff. The foundation's 501(c)(3) caption is the face. 5% can include administrative costs and some program-related investments; corpus remains.

This page does not decide whether any foundation's grants were good. It names who keeps the pile.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a lawful dynasty of deducted capital. The parties who obtain it are private-foundation managers and the families that appoint them. Analog recipients take the annual slice, if they are chosen.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A public charity that must pass a support test ([pla-062](501c3-franchise-vs-mutual-aid.md)). A DAF with **no** 5% duty ([pla-068](donor-advised-funds-no-payout.md)) — the later, looser cousin. The closed analog is a required spend-down, or no deduction until a working charity spends the money.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** 1969 is sold as constraint. It is also the code that makes perpetual private foundations the standard estate plan for large deducted wealth.

Carnegie Corporation, Rockefeller Foundation, Ford Foundation are application of first-receiver wealth ([pla-029](fiat-first-receiver-impulse.md), [pla-032](hardrock-mining-patent-1872.md) adjacent) into a policy desk. Routing, not personality.

## Routing

Enabled: 501(c)(3) private foundation; 5% and excises as the compliance track. Closed: deduction only upon expenditure on a person; mandatory sunset.

## Application Evidence

§ 4942's 5% is the mechanism. FoundationMark / NPT comparisons: private-foundation assets on the order of **$1.48 trillion** with grants/expenditures about **$114 billion** (2023, as cited in NPT 2024 DAF report). Scope: that report's year and definitions — not a library audit of every 990-PF.

Payout that includes overhead is structural. Do not invent a "true charitable" percentage.
