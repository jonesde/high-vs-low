# Tax-funded exclusive school

Compulsory days ([pla-079](compulsory-attendance.md)) are funded by a **default purse**: local property millage plus state aid to a designated local education agency (LEA). The analog parent pays whether or not the child sits in that school. *Pierce* preserved a private school as a **second bill**, not a redirect of the first. [pla-060](faith-based-public-money.md) is the later overlay in which faith-based **providers take** public money (*Trinity Lutheran*, *Espinoza*, *Carson*). This page is the **compulsory default**: you pay for the franchise school even if you use another. Vouchers and education savings accounts are a partial analog opening — mention as a crack, not the thesis.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a tax-funded exclusive desk. The parties who obtain it are LEAs, their unions and vendors, and the bondholders on school plant. Parents who instruct elsewhere take the residual: they still pay, and they still count days under [pla-079](compulsory-attendance.md) / [pla-082](homeschool-umbrella-capture.md).

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Parent directs the tax share to a chosen instructor. A complete opt-out of the millage. The closed analog is the gift to a person or a shop as the educational event ([pla-061](charitable-deduction-first-receiver.md) cousin: deduction runs to the qualifying org, not the neighbor).

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** "Free public school" is free at the door and compulsory at the levy. *Carson v. Makin* later said that if a state funds any private school it must include religious ones — that is [pla-060](faith-based-public-money.md), a fight over **which franchisee** gets a slice, not over whether the analog parent keeps the purse.

## Routing

Enabled: administrative levy and aid to the LEA. Closed: parent assignment of the tax share to a named instructor.

## Application Evidence

Every state's millage-plus-aid stack is the mechanism. *Rodriguez* is the federal holding that inequality of that purse is not a federal equal-protection problem. State adequacy suits (e.g. *Campaign for Fiscal Equity* in New York; *Abbott* in New Jersey) fight **inside** the franchise: more money to the designated desk, not a redirect to the parent.

Homeschool legalization of recent decades ([pla-082](homeschool-umbrella-capture.md)) opened an instructional analog without opening the purse. That is the improvement and the remaining wall.

## Framework notes
