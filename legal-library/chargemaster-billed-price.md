# Chargemaster billed price

A hospital chargemaster is the list of **gross charges** for items and services. Actual amounts paid depend on status: Medicare fee schedule, insurer contract, uninsured, or 501(r) financial-assistance ([pla-070](operating-charity-capital-pool.md) — exemption versus duty, not this page). CMS hospital price-transparency rules, 45 C.F.R. Part 180 (2019 rule, in force 2021), require hospitals to post standard charges, including the chargemaster gross charge. The analog cash-pay clinic posts a price that is the price. The No Surprises Act (Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021, Pub. L. 116-260, generally effective Jan. 1, 2022) limits some out-of-network surprise bills; it is a crack, not a posted-price market. This page is **price as first-receiver extraction**. It is not gene-method technique control ([pla-022](gene-method-patents.md)) and not PREP immunity ([pla-042](covered-person-liability-shields.md)).

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a billed amount that is not a market price. The parties who obtain it are hospitals (nonprofit and for-profit) and the insurers who negotiate off the list. Analog patients take the residual: a gross charge, a "discount," or a collection.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A cash-pay independent practitioner whose posted price is the price. The closed analog is the same service at one amount regardless of insurer status. 501(r) limits charges to FAP-eligible patients at a 501(c)(3) hospital — a residual duty beside the list, not a repeal of the list.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** Transparency rules require posting the chargemaster. They do not make the gross charge the price anyone with an insurer pays. The analog uninsured person historically saw the list; insured status is the franchise.

## Routing

Enabled: contractual insurer rates off the list; administrative posting of the list; civil collection of a billed amount. Closed: posted cash price as the price.

## Application Evidence

Part 180's definition of **gross charge** as the chargemaster amount is the mechanism. CMS compliance reviews and civil monetary penalties for non-posting police the **disclosure**, not the level of the list. Scope: the rule. Do not invent a national average markup.

No Surprises Act is date-labeled: a 2022 overlay on some out-of-network facility and air-ambulance bills. It does not convert the chargemaster into a cash market. Facility fees on outpatient departments versus independent clinics are a status-dependent cousin: same service, different billed entity.
