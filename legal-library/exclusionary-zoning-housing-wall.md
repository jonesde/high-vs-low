# Exclusionary zoning as housing wall

*Village of Euclid v. Ambler Realty*, 272 U.S. 365 (1926), upheld a zoning ordinance that kept apartment houses out of detached-house districts as an exercise of the police power. *Village of Arlington Heights v. Metropolitan Housing Development Corp.*, 429 U.S. 252 (1977), held that racially disproportionate **impact** is not enough to strike a refusal to rezone for multifamily housing; the plaintiff must show discriminatory **intent**. Together they are the constitutional wall: a jurisdiction may reserve land for incumbent dwellings and lose only if someone proves purpose. This page is **who may dwell**. [pla-009](zoning-incumbent-protection.md) is who may operate a shop — variance, grandfather, complaint-driven tickets. Do not collapse them.

William Fischel's account of a 1970s zoning revolution (homevoters; interstate-era growth controls; suburban downzoning after the baby boom filled) is the secondary history of **when** the wall tightened at metropolitan scale. The analog is as-of-right extra dwelling, duplex, or manufactured siting.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is protection of existing owners' house values and neighborhood composition by keeping lower-cost dwellings out. The parties who obtain it are incumbent homeowners and the boards they elect. Would-be residents and lower-cost builders take the residual: delay, denial, or a different jurisdiction.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

As-of-right construction of a second unit, a duplex, or a HUD-code home on a lot that already has water and a road. The closed analog is treating additional dwellings as the same use as the house already there. [pla-009](zoning-incumbent-protection.md) incumbents who already hold a nonconforming shop are a different fight.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** Facially general "health, safety, morals, and general welfare" language. *Euclid* itself treated apartments as a nuisance next to detached houses. *Arlington Heights* then required intent, so impact on who can live there is not the official harm.

## Formal Legal Instruments

| Instrument | Role in the outcome | Citation |

## Routing

Enabled: administrative refusal of density; civil defense of the map. Closed: as-of-right additional dwelling under the same residential use.

## Application Evidence

*Euclid* and *Arlington Heights* are the holdings. Fischel, *The Homevoter Hypothesis* (2001) and "An Economic History of Zoning" (*Urban Studies*, 2004): around 1970, completed interstates and mobile jobs led suburbs to adopt growth controls; homeowners became the political principals of the map. Scope: that account, not a national grant-rate table.

Local zoning-atlas and city studies repeatedly find detached single-family exclusive zoning as the dominant residential category in sampled metros. Cite a named city or atlas year; do not invent a national statutory percentage.

Manufactured-home siting is the double wall with [pla-075](building-code-hud-manufactured-floor.md): HUD Code may preempt construction standards; local zoning still says where the home may sit.
