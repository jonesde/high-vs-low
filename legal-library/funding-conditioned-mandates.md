# Funding-conditioned and workplace health mandates

The National Minimum Drinking Age Act (1984) does not set a national drinking age. It withholds 10% of highway funds from states that do not prohibit purchase of alcohol under 21. OSHA's 2021 vaccine-or-test ETS for large employers and CMS's staff-vaccination condition on Medicare/Medicaid participation used workplace and payment rules the same way. *NFIB v. OSHA* stayed the ETS; *Biden v. Missouri* upheld the CMS condition. The analog is a person saying no to a product or a drink without losing a job, a license, or a state's roads money.

This page does not decide medical efficacy. It names **compulsion by purse and by workplace**.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a choice that is not a choice: take the mandated product or lose the federally touched status. The parties who obtain it are the federal agencies that write the condition and the firms/hospitals that enforce it as HR policy. Workers, students, and states that want the money take the residual.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

An adult refusing a drink after 1933. Informed consent for an ordinary drug, with the right to walk away. The closed analog is keeping the job or the grant after "no."

**Other complex:** highway funds have long been the MIC/infrastructure purse for social policy (seat belts, speed limits, 21 drinking age). The health complex reused the template for COVID staff rules. Defense-contractor vaccine conditions are the MIC overlap.

## Formal Legal Instruments

| Instrument | Role in the outcome | Citation |
| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- |
| National Minimum Drinking Age Act | 10% highway-fund penalty unless the state bans under-21 purchase | [23 U.S.C. § 158](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/23/158); *South Dakota v. Dole*, 483 U.S. 203 (1987) |
| OSHA ETS (2021) | Vaccine-or-test for employers with 100+ employees | OSHA COVID-19 Vaccination and Testing ETS; stayed in *NFIB v. OSHA*, 595 U.S. 109 (2022) |
| CMS staff mandate | Vaccination as a condition of participation | *Biden v. Missouri*, 595 U.S. 87 (2022) (upheld) |
| Contractor / federal-employee rules | Separate executive conditions on federal workplaces | Executive orders, later litigated |

*Dole* is the spending-power hinge: Congress may condition funds if related to the spending's purpose. Highway money for a drinking age is the purity-control ancestor of CMS money for a staff shot.

## Routing

Enabled: spending conditions, workplace ETS, conditions of participation. Closed: a direct national criminal command (Volstead) or a truly unconditioned job.

Pair with [pla-042](covered-person-liability-shields.md): the product one is compelled to take is often the product one cannot sue over.

## Application Evidence

*Dole* upheld § 158. Every state eventually complied. That is compulsion of states, then of young drinkers.

*NFIB*: the Court treated the OSHA ETS as exceeding workplace-safety authority for a public-health vaccine rule of that breadth. *Missouri*: CMS conditions of participation were inside HHS's statute. The split is **which purse**. Hospital workers lost; many private-sector workers under the ETS did not, because of the caption of the agency.

University and employer policies that copied federal guidance without a statute are the contractual overlay. They are not PREP. They are still the complex's enforcement layer.
