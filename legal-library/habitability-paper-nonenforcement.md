# Habitability paper non-enforcement

*Javins v. First National Realty*, 428 F.2d 1071 (D.C. Cir. 1970), treated an urban lease as carrying an implied warranty of habitability: the tenant's duty to pay rent is tied to the landlord's duty to keep the premises livable. Most states followed by decision or statute. Local housing codes already forbade the same conditions. Enforcement is **discretionary** against the landlord (complaint-driven inspectors, finite crews, settlements) and often **strict** against the analog occupant of an unpermitted or "substandard" dwelling ([pla-075](building-code-hud-manufactured-floor.md)). The analog is tenant repair-and-deduct as of right, or an owner-occupant of an analog shack not ticketed while the landlord's building is left.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a paper right that does not reliably produce heat, plumbing, or a withheld rent. The parties who obtain it are landlords who can wait out inspectors and still use [pla-076](eviction-summary-process.md). Tenants take the residual: a warranty to plead, a code to call, and a lockout calendar that runs faster than the repair.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Repair-and-deduct or rent withholding that actually stays the writ. Even-handed inspection of owner-occupied and rental stock. The closed analog is treating the housing code as a duty that stops possession until the repair, not a ticket that may or may not issue.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** The code is mandatory. The inspector's time is not. Occupancy of a tent, a vehicle, or an unpermitted unit is a quick ticket; a landlord's open violation next door continues on a complaint queue.

## Routing

Enabled: civil warranty as a defense or counterclaim; administrative inspection if someone complains. Closed: repair as a condition of keeping the rent or the writ; even-handed occupancy rules for analog owner-occupants.

Non-action is part of the design: no inspector, no case.

## Application Evidence

*Javins* is the doctrinal turn. Local variation is the evidence type, not a defect: complaint-driven inspection plus finite crews is structural. Do not invent a national inspection-failure rate.

Receivership and "repair-and-deduct" statutes are the crack. They still sit inside a summary eviction case in many states. Code enforcement against unhoused or extra-occupancy analog people is the other face of the same ordinance ([pla-009](zoning-incumbent-protection.md) selective tickets).
