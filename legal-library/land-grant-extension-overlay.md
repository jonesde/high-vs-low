# Land-grant extension overlay

The Morrill Act of 1862 granted public-domain land (or scrip) to the states to endow colleges of agriculture and the mechanic arts. The Morrill Act of 1890 added cash for a second designated college in states that excluded Black students from the first. The Hatch Act of 1887 funded agricultural experiment stations; the Smith-Lever Act of 1914 funded cooperative extension. Independent apprenticeship, farmer knowledge, and un-endowed mechanical instruction are the analog. This page ties [pla-036](land-grants-and-tariff-incidence.md) (public domain to franchisees) to education. Bayh-Dole ([pla-019](bayh-dole-exclusive-license.md)) is the later exclusive residual on what those desks invent.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a designated college and extension desk between people and agricultural/mechanical knowledge, funded with land and federal money. The parties who obtain it are land-grant universities and the USDA extension system. Analog local instructors and un-endowed shops take the residual: no land grant, no Hatch station, no Smith-Lever agent.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Local apprenticeship; independent agricultural knowledge; a school without a public-domain endowment. The closed analog is treating instruction in farming and trades as a commons rather than a designated campus. 1890 colleges are a second franchise created because the first excluded — a crack inside the designation, not an analog opening.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** "The people's college" is the caption. The land and the annual federal money are the franchise. Extension that teaches the analog farmer still runs through the designated agent.

## Routing

Enabled: administrative grant of land and annual federal funds to the designated campus. Closed: analog local instruction as a public-domain residual.

## Application Evidence

The four statutes are the mechanism. Every state has at least one 1862 land-grant; 1890 institutions are the second designated list. Extension's county-agent system is the live overlay: USDA money through the campus to the county. Scope: the statutes, not a verdict on whether any station's research was useful.

Homestead-scale entry ([pla-036](land-grants-and-tariff-incidence.md)) was the analog land path; Morrill was the college path on the same public domain. That is the split.
