# Self-funding forfeiture as drug-war revenue

[pla-002](civil-asset-forfeiture.md) is the general taking machine. This page is the **Drug War overlay**: the Comprehensive Crime Control Act of 1984 expanded civil forfeiture and equitable sharing so state and local agencies could adopt federal drug forfeiture and keep a large share of proceeds. Enforcement that pays itself does not need a tax increase. It needs seizures. The analog is a police budget voted by a legislature, with no residual claim on the suspect's cash.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a positive-feedback loop: more drug stops, more cash and cars, more gear and overtime ([pla-040](militarized-local-enforcement.md)). The parties who obtain it are participating task forces and prosecutors. Low-cash, high-stop neighborhoods take the seizures, often without a conviction ([pla-002](civil-asset-forfeiture.md) evidence).

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A criminal forfeiture after conviction. A city council appropriation. Pharma civil penalties that go to Treasury, not to the inspecting office's toys. The closed analog is drug policing that cannot keep the loot.

**Other complex:** PREP/CICP ([pla-042](covered-person-liability-shields.md)) is the inverse money flow — the designated product-maker is shielded and the public fund, if any, is small. Forfeiture is extraction from the outside; PREP is immunity for the inside. Both finance the complex without a general tax vote on the specific use.

## Formal Legal Instruments

| Instrument | Role in the outcome | Citation |
| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- |
| CCCA 1984 | Broadened civil forfeiture and DOJ's Assets Forfeiture Fund | Pub. L. 98-473 |
| 21 U.S.C. § 881 | Drug-related civil forfeiture of conveyances, money, real property | [21 U.S.C. § 881](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/21/881) |
| Equitable sharing | Local agencies adopt federal forfeiture and receive up to ~80% of proceeds | DOJ/Treasury sharing guides; [pla-002](civil-asset-forfeiture.md) |
| CAFRA 2000 | Some procedural limits; sharing remains | 18 U.S.C. § 983 |

## Routing

Enabled: civil *in rem* forfeiture in drug cases; administrative default; sharing that bypasses stricter state law. Closed: conviction as a condition of keeping the property; legislative control of the enforcement budget.

## Application Evidence

See [pla-002](civil-asset-forfeiture.md) for IJ *Policing for Profit* volumes and Philadelphia contestation. This page adds the 1984 timing: forfeiture expansion is a Drug War statute, not a general property-tax reform. OJP's CCCA description lists forfeiture of assets among the bill's main sections alongside drug penalties and federal grants.
