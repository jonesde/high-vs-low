# Operating charity as capital pool

A nonprofit hospital or university is a 501(c)(3). It holds endowment, land, and tax-exempt bonds. Patients still receive bills; students still pay tuition. ACA-added § 501(r) requires hospital community-health-needs assessments, financial-assistance policies, and limits on certain charges and collections. Form 990 Schedule H reports "community benefit" (charity care at cost, Medicaid shortfalls, education, research). The IRS has **not** set a required community-benefit percentage. Analog: a taxable hospital or school; analog patient/student who still pays. This page is **exemption versus duty**, not healthcare billing-as-price ([pla-085](chargemaster-billed-price.md)) and not certificate-of-need entry walls ([pla-086](certificate-of-need.md)).

Controllers: systems, boards, bondholders. Charity is the caption. Religious hospital systems overlap [pla-060](faith-based-public-money.md).

This page does not decide how much free care is "enough." It names the residual duty beside exempt capital.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is an exempt capital pool with a reporting overlay. The parties who obtain it are 501(c)(3) hospital and university corporations. Analog patients and students take financial-assistance policies and sticker prices.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A for-profit hospital that pays property and income tax. A municipal hospital. A university without a large endowment. The closed analog is exemption conditioned on a hard spend (a percent of surplus on charity care; a tuition cap tied to endowment). State property-tax challenges that revoke exemption when charity care is thin are the crack; label by state.

| Schedule H | Public report of community benefit; no IRS minimum percent | Form 990 Schedule H |
| UPMIFA | Endowment spending at universities and hospital foundations | [pla-069](upmifa-endowment-lock.md) |

Rev. Rul. 69-545 (community-benefit standard replacing a "relief of poverty" hospital test) is the older IRS costume. 501(r) added process, not a floor.

## Routing

Enabled: exemption; Schedule H reporting; 501(r) policies. Closed: a justiciable right to care or to a tuition set by endowment size.

## Application Evidence

CRS R48027: IRS has not specified a community-benefit amount or percentage; Schedule H is the report. TIGTA (2025) has criticized Schedule H as hard to use for assessing benefit — cite as oversight of the residual duty, not as a library verdict on any hospital. University endowment-study spending rates belong on [pla-069](upmifa-endowment-lock.md); this page adds the operating-company wrapper.

Do not invent a national charity-care-to-exemption-value ratio. State case studies (property-tax revocation fights) are illustrations.
