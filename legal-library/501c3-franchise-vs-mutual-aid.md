# 501(c)(3) franchise vs mutual aid

"Charitable" in ordinary speech means help. In the Code it means an organization organized and operated exclusively for listed purposes, no private inurement, and (for public charities) a support test under § 509. Grantmakers, payroll systems, and § 170 receipts require an EIN and a determination letter. Informal mutual aid — a circle that actually feeds people, a burial society, a neighbor fund — is the analog that does the work and cannot receive the deductible wire.

Controllers: counsel who designs the org, boards who hold title, foundations and DAFs that will only grant to a 501(c)(3). The people helped are not members of the franchise unless the org says so.

Churches are a special 501(c)(3) that need not apply or file a 990 ([pla-052](church-tax-opacity.md)). This page is the **non-church** franchise and the exclusion of unincorporated aid.

This page does not decide whether incorporation is wise. It names who counts as charity.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a gated definition. The parties who obtain it are organizations that pass the tests. Mutual-aid circles, unregistered churches-that-are-not-churches, and person-to-person help take the residual: taxable or non-deductible.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A GoFundMe for a named person (generally not a § 170 donee). A rotating savings club. A union strike fund. The closed analog is treating the act of feeding someone as the charitable event without an operational-test org in between.

**Other complex:** licensing monopoly on the analog molecule ([pla-044](licensing-monopoly-on-the-analog.md)) — a number that makes the same object lawful. Here a determination letter makes the same gift deductible.

Private inurement and excess benefit police **who inside the org may take**. They do not restore standing to the analog beneficiary ([pla-064](ag-only-charitable-standing.md)).

## Routing

Enabled: Form 1023, determination letter, 990, deductible receipts. Closed: analog circle as donee; grantmakers wiring to a natural person as "charity."

## Application Evidence

The Code's definition is the mechanism. Community-chest and foundation grant guidelines that require 501(c)(3) status are application of **who may be paid**. IRS determination inventories (EO BMF) count franchisees; they do not count mutual aid. Say so.

Scientific charity ([pla-072](scientific-charity-means-test-gate.md)) professionalized the gate before the 1954 Code restatement. 501(c)(3) is the later tax costume of that professional org.
