# Designated education finance

Instruction is the analog. The money is a franchise. The Servicemen's Readjustment Act of 1944 (GI Bill) and later Title 38 pay education costs for a **designated veteran** at an approved school. Higher Education Act Title IV ([pla-081](accreditation-title-iv-wall.md)) pays for a **designated accredited-student**. Church revolving funds and mission scholarships pay for a **designated member**. The analog is an ungated loan or gift for instruction as such. This page is the extra purse. [pla-080](tax-funded-exclusive-school.md) is the compulsory K-12 levy; [pla-060](faith-based-public-money.md) is which provider may take public money.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is human-capital finance that runs through a status gate. The parties who obtain it are approved schools, lenders, and the institutions that set the gate (VA, Education Department, a church). People who want instruction without that status take cash or nothing.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A loan or gift that follows the student to any instructor. The closed analog is treating instruction, not veteran/accredited/recommend status, as the event.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** Education finance is sold as opportunity. The gate is **who may be the debtor or the donee**. ROTC and service academies are the military cousin: designated training in exchange for service, not a general scholarship.

## Routing

Enabled: administrative eligibility plus contract with an approved school. Closed: ungated finance of instruction.

## Application Evidence

**Public designated money (lead).** GI Bill scale is the large US franchise: VA education benefits at approved institutions. Title IV is the larger civilian cousin. ROTC and the service academies are a second campus pipeline: designated federal education in exchange for a service obligation — not unique to any church.

**Church-gated revolving funds (one illustration among others).** The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints' Perpetual Education Fund (announced 2001, Hinckley) makes revolving loans for members, typically outside the United States and Canada, for education expected to lead to better employment. Church materials require a **current temple recommend** among other conditions. Church reporting at the 25-year mark: on the order of **130,000** students, loans/scholarships used in **more than 80 countries**, program then available in 76. Scope: those church figures. Catholic university mission scholarships with faith requirements are a parallel **non-LDS** church gate: status, not instruction as such.

**BYU-Pathway Worldwide** is a related remote-certificate/degree path. BYU-Pathway's 2024 annual report: **74,839** students; **37%** in Africa; Africa as the largest region; 180 countries. Later church/Pathway releases have cited still higher Africa shares. Scope: those releases. Pathway and PEF are church-gated human-capital pipelines (endorsed careers, remote study, remaining in-country in many tellings). They are not a claim about intelligence agencies in Africa.

**US campus recruiting (separate from Africa).** BYU-Provo FBI/CIA/NSA recruiting is a **reported US campus pattern**, not a conspiracy page: *Ramparts* (1971); Associated Press (1981); Atlas Obscura (2015); **Deseret News (2022)** describing a "more nuanced" present (mission-language skills, low-risk lifestyle, cheaper clearance — the paper's framing). Ecclesiastical endorsement in BYU hiring is a campus HR fact where sourced. ROTC and service-academy pipelines are the second, non-LDS campus example so BYU is not unique-by-insinuation. Do not merge this paragraph with Pathway's Africa enrollment.
