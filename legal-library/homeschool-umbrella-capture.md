# Homeschool umbrella capture

Compulsory attendance ([pla-079](compulsory-attendance.md)) later grew statutory exceptions: homeschool. The analog — instruction at home without a covering corporation — is legal only inside those statutes. Some states require notice, hours, testing, or a teaching credential. Some have no standalone homeschool option: the family must enroll in a **church school or private-school cover** (Alabama's church-school path is the long-standing illustration; other states have umbrella academies). Homeschool Legal Defense Association (HSLDA) is the analog movement's own legal franchise: membership, forms, and a covering theory. *Wisconsin v. Yoder*, 406 U.S. 205 (1972), already on [pla-057](faith-healing-child-exemptions.md), is the religious overlay after eighth grade — a crack, not a general homeschool right. This page is the exception **and** the capture: some alternatives become another centralized cluster, or part of a captured religious cluster.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is that family instruction is legal as a **status** (registered homeschool, church-school enrollee, umbrella student), not as of right. The parties who obtain it are state education agencies, covering schools, and defense organizations. Families who instruct without the cover take truancy process.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Instruction at home with no notice, no covering corporation, and no hours ledger. The closed analog is treating the family as a school without filing. Recent-decade legalization is a real opening; the remaining wall is the cover, the hours, and the cluster.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** Homeschool is sold as exit from the LEA ([pla-080](tax-funded-exclusive-school.md)). In cover states, exit is **enrollment in a different franchise**. Accreditation of a homeschool co-op as a private school is the same shape as [pla-081](accreditation-title-iv-wall.md) at small scale.

## Routing

Enabled: administrative exception via registration or cover. Closed: family instruction without a statutory school.

## Application Evidence

State statutory variation is the evidence type. Alabama (and historically similar cover states) is the illustration that "homeschool" may mean **church-school enrollment**. HSLDA's state law map is a practitioner catalog of those gates, not a second legal system. Scope: the statute of the named state.

Capture: a homeschool co-op that becomes an accredited private school, or a cover academy that sets curriculum and reporting, is the analog movement recoded as a cluster. Do not treat every co-op as captured; name the mechanism (cover, accreditation, hours).
