# Scientific charity means-test gate

Buffalo's Charity Organization Society (December 1877), copied from London (1869) by S. Humphreys Gurteen, announced it would **give no relief from its own funds** except in rare emergencies. Signs: "No Relief Given Here." The work was investigation, a clearinghouse of applications, and coordination of existing agencies so "indiscriminate" alms would stop. Associated Charities in other cities followed. The analog is direct aid without a file — a neighbor, a parish, a burial society. The people the movement claimed to protect were sorted into deserving and undeserving. Many were denied.

There is no single federal COS statute. The instruments **are** the charters, municipal ordinances that handed outdoor-relief intake to police or COS investigators, and the later professional nonprofit that inherited the gate. Say so. 501(c)(3) ([pla-062](501c3-franchise-vs-mutual-aid.md)) is the tax costume; federated chests ([pla-065](federated-workplace-capture.md)) are the fundraising costume. Carnegie *Gospel of Wealth* is a **stance** of first-receiver philanthropy teaching the poor, not a finding that the gospel is true.

Controllers: COS boards, businessmen-reformers, later professional social work. The poor person is the object of the file.

This page does not decide that all eligibility rules are cruel. It names the inversion: **the process outranks the alms**.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a gate. The parties who obtain it are investigating charities and municipal relief offices that adopted the method. Applicants take the residual: a case record, a delay, a no.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Alms without investigation. Mutual aid inside a lodge or congregation. The closed analog is treating need as sufficient. Later TANF and charitable-choice intake ([pla-060](faith-based-public-money.md)) reuse eligibility files; that purse is a different page. This page is the private-charity origin of the file.

**Other complex:** tax-schedule-as-crime ([pla-038](tax-schedule-as-crime.md)) — administrative classification that makes the analog a problem. Here classification makes the person undeserving.

## Routing

Enabled: investigation, central registry, refusal as "scientific." Closed: indiscriminate alms as legitimate charity; analog person aided without a file.

## Application Evidence

Buffalo COS founding meeting 11 Dec. 1877; "No Relief Given Here" as stated policy (Hudson Institute / COS histories). Diffusion: Boston Associated Charities and other cities in the 1880s. Cleveland COS 1881 sits beside the later 1913 chest and 1914 foundation — one city, three layers of impersonal charity.

Do not invent a national denial rate. The preferential fact is the **gate as the product**: COS sold investigation, not bread.
