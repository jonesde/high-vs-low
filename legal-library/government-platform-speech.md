# Government-platform speech routing

[pla-012](platform-safe-harbors.md) is the private stack: § 230 plus terms of service. This page is the **official overlay**. Government may speak. *Bantam Books* (1963) and *NRA v. Vullo* (2024) say it may not coerce a private intermediary to suppress someone else's speech. COVID-era White House, CDC, Surgeon General, and CISA contacts with platforms are the large modern application: flags, "misinformation" portals, and takedown requests about a covered countermeasure ([pla-042](covered-person-liability-shields.md)) and a mandate ([pla-043](funding-conditioned-mandates.md)). *Murthy v. Missouri* (2024) ended that particular injunction on **standing**. It did not hold that jawboning is lawful, and it did not hold that it is not. The analog is a person saying the same sentence on a sidewalk, or printing it, without a platform ToS and without an agency flag.

This page does not decide virology or product efficacy. It names **who can make a statement costly to host**.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is removal, throttling, or account loss for speech that contradicts the complex's official record ([pla-046](captured-evidence-associations.md)), without a First Amendment judgment against the official on the merits. The parties who obtain it are agencies that want a narrative and platforms that want § 230, procurement, and regulatory peace. Users keep a ToS appeal.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A soapbox. A newspaper that is the publisher and can be sued as one. Government's own speech (a CDC webpage), which the First Amendment does not freeze. The closed analog is a court order that a platform must carry the dissent, or a damages action against the official who demanded the takedown, tried on the *Bantam Books* / *Vullo* line.

**Other complex:** intelligence and homeland-security "disinformation" partnerships (CISA, FBI foreign-influence flags) use the same intermediary. Defense and intel complexes already had information operations; the health complex reused the pipe for a domestic product narrative.

## Formal Legal Instruments

| Instrument | Role in the outcome | Citation |
| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- |
| First Amendment | Bars state coercion of private speakers and, under *Bantam Books*, of intermediaries | U.S. Const. amend. I |
| *Bantam Books* | Informal notice-plus-threat to distributors is unconstitutional prior restraint | *Bantam Books, Inc. v. Sullivan*, 372 U.S. 58 (1963) |
| *Vullo* | Plausible claim where a regulator coerces firms to cut off a disfavored speaker | *National Rifle Association of America v. Vullo*, 602 U.S. 175 (2024) |
| *Murthy* | COVID/election jawboning suit; no Article III standing for the requested injunction | *Murthy v. Missouri*, 603 U.S. 43 (2024) |
| § 230 stack | Platform is not the publisher; ToS lets it drop the post | [47 U.S.C. § 230](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/230); [pla-012](platform-safe-harbors.md) |
| Surgeon General advisory (2021) | Official call for algorithmic demotion and consequences for "super-spreaders" | HHS, *Confronting Health Misinformation* (2021) (discussed in *Murthy*) |

The *Missouri* district court and Fifth Circuit found a likely First Amendment violation and issued (then narrowed) an injunction. The Supreme Court reversed on standing. The record of government–platform contacts remains in that docket and in later congressional exhibits. Label those as **documents**, not as a Supreme Court merits holding.

## Routing

Enabled: government speech and "requests"; platform contract; § 230 immunity for the takedown. Closed: a merits judgment that this official coerced this removal of this speaker, in the *Murthy* posture.

*Vullo* is the live coercion cause of action. *Murthy* is the standing wall in the COVID fact pattern the Court was given. Preferential application is the **gap**: the health-complex speech operation was not adjudicated; a gun-advocacy intermediary claim was.

## Application Evidence

*Murthy*'s majority recounts White House, CDC, Surgeon General, and FBI communications with platforms about COVID and election content. Scope: that record, as characterized in a standing opinion. The 2021 Surgeon General advisory recommended algorithmic changes and consequences for "super-spreaders." House Judiciary / "Twitter Files" productions (2022–23) are primary documents of internal flags; they are not findings of law.

*Vullo* (unanimous on the pleading standard) is the contrast case that did reach coercion doctrine the same Term.
