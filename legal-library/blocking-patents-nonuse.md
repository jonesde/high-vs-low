# Blocking patents and non-use

US patent law does not require the owner to practice the invention. *Continental Paper Bag* (1908) allowed an injunction at the request of a patentee who had not built the machine. Equipped competitors — including generics with factories and implementers with plants — still need a license. This is **suppression and non-use**, not PAE rent extraction ([pla-004](patent-assertion-entities.md)). It is also not drug-price-as-poverty. The mechanism is an exclusive right that need not be worked.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is that a technique remains unused, or used only by the owner's leave, even when others have the capital and know-how to practice it. The parties who obtain it are patentees who sit on the right, including operating companies blocking substitutes and owners who prefer the old process. Practitioners without a license are infringers.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Some jurisdictions condition patents on working, or provide routine compulsory licenses (Paris Convention art. 5A; TRIPS art. 31 as a bounded safety valve). US law has no general working requirement and almost no compulsory licensing outside narrow pockets (Bayh-Dole march-in, unused; 28 U.S.C. § 1498 government use; some Clean Air and atomic-energy provisions). A practicing competitor using the same technique without a US patent in force is the analog that the exclusive right closes.

[pla-004](patent-assertion-entities.md) is assertion against operators to extract settlement. This page is the unworked exclusive right as a veto on practice.

## Formal Legal Instruments

| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- |
| Exclusive rights not conditioned on working | Make, use, sell, offer, import — no working clause | [35 U.S.C. § 154](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/35/154), [§ 271](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/35/271) |

| Unused / rare safety valves | March-in; government use; isolated statutory compulsory licenses | 35 U.S.C. § 203; 28 U.S.C. § 1498 |
| TRIPS / Paris contrast | Working requirements and compulsory licenses exist in the international text as options | Paris art. 5A; TRIPS art. 31 |

## Routing

Enabled: civil infringement and (often) injunction or exclusion order. Closed: a regular administrative compulsory license for non-working.

*eBay* made injunctions discretionary, which helps some defendants with lawyers. It is not a working requirement. PAEs after *eBay* still extract; operating patentees still veto.

## Application Evidence

**Doctrine.** *Continental Paper Bag*: Eastern held a patent on a paper-bag machine it had not commercialized; Continental practiced a similar machine. The Court affirmed an injunction. Non-use was not a defense. That is still the baseline: the right to exclude does not depend on the owner using the technique.

**Safety valves in non-use.** March-in: never exercised (see [pla-019](bayh-dole-exclusive-license.md)). § 1498 government-use is a takings-style work-around, not a general compulsory license for competitors.

Do not fill this page with unsourced "suppressed inventions" folklore. The citable preferential fact is the legal structure: equipped third parties are closed out whether or not the owner works the patent.
