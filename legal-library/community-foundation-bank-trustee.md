# Community foundation as bank trustee

Frederick Goff and the Cleveland Trust Company created the Cleveland Foundation in 1914: a **community trust**. Financial assets sit in bank trusts; a distribution committee makes grants. Boston, Chicago, and others copied it. Today's community foundations still hold perpetual funds, often including donor-advised accounts ([pla-068](donor-advised-funds-no-payout.md)), under UPMIFA prudence ([pla-069](upmifa-endowment-lock.md)). The analog is a local gift that is spent on a named need this year.

Controllers: trust companies and civic boards. The foundation is the face. Goff's design was explicit: leverage "disparate philanthropic assets into a single endowment." Perpetuity is the point.

This page does not decide whether community foundations do useful grants. It names who holds the corpus and who allocates.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a permanent pool under bank and committee control. The parties who obtain it are trustees and distribution committees. Analog living recipients take whatever the committee grants, when it grants.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A spent gift to a pantry. A private foundation the family still names ([pla-067](private-foundation-perpetual-control.md)) — cousin, not contrast. The closed analog is a community chest that **distributes the campaign** rather than endows it. Cleveland ran both: the 1913 federation spent; the 1914 foundation endowed. That split is the arc.

## Formal Legal Instruments

| Instrument | Role in the outcome | Citation |
| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- |
| Community trust / bank trustee | Perpetual funds held by a trust company | Cleveland Foundation indenture (1914) and copies |
| 501(c)(3) community foundation | Tax-exempt public-charity wrapper | 26 U.S.C. §§ 501(c)(3), 509 |
| UPMIFA | Prudent spending and modification of institutional funds | UPMIFA (2006) as enacted |
| Component DAFs | Donor-advised accounts inside the foundation | [pla-068](donor-advised-funds-no-payout.md) |

No single federal "community foundation statute." The preferential design is **trust + public charity + committee**.

## Routing

Enabled: perpetual trust; committee grants; DAF subaccounts. Closed: mandatory spend-down; analog person as remainder.

## Application Evidence

Cleveland Foundation 1914 is the template (Goff; Cleveland Trust as trustee). Ballotpedia and foundation histories of Boston, Chicago, Minneapolis, Milwaukee, Buffalo following within five years are diffusion evidence. UPMIFA later nationalized the spending law.

Do not invent a national community-foundation AUM figure without SOI/CF Insights year. Structure is the evidence until a scoped table is added.
