# Captured evidence and professional associations

The Controlled Substances Act tells DEA to weigh "scientific evidence," "the state of current scientific knowledge," and "currently accepted medical use" when it schedules a substance ([pla-038](tax-schedule-as-crime.md)). Those phrases are not self-executing. FDA review clocks and advisory committees, CDC/ACIP recommendations, specialty-society guidelines, and medical-board "standard of care" supply the official facts. The Prescription Drug User Fee Act (1992) lets the industry that wants approval **pay a large share of the review budget**. The analog is independent evidence — a clinician series, an off-label observation, a trial the sponsor did not fund — treated as medical practice or as "misinformation," not as the record that moves a schedule or a license.

This page does not decide whether any particular drug, vaccine, or guideline was correct. It names **who is paid to produce the record the complex treats as true**.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a gated epistemology. The parties who obtain it are user-fee payors, professional associations whose guidelines boards adopt, and agencies whose performance is scored on review speed. Uncredentialed or dissenting practitioners, and patients who want a molecule the guild has not blessed, take the residual: discipline, scheduling, or platform removal ([pla-047](government-platform-speech.md)).

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A university lab publishing a method without an association imprimatur. Off-label prescribing, which is lawful for a physician and a marketing crime for a company — the guild still holds the pen. A CSA petition to reschedule that loses because DEA finds no "accepted medical use" while licensed analog products remain on other schedules ([pla-044](licensing-monopoly-on-the-analog.md)). The closed analog is treating independently generated clinical evidence as sufficient to practice, to reschedule, or to warn, without the fee-and-association gate.

**Other complex:** Nationally Recognized Statistical Rating Organizations in finance (the designated raters of credit). Military specifications written with the contractors who sell to the spec. "Finished intelligence" as the official record versus open-source work that contradicts it. In each case the complex designates who may mint evidence.

ICH (regulator–industry harmonization) and specialty-society guidelines (IDSA, AAP, and others) are not statutes. They become law when an agency or a board treats them as the record.

## Routing

Enabled: administrative review funded by the applicant; association guidelines; board discipline; scheduling that cites the same record. Closed: a hearing in which independent evidence binds the agency the way a jury binds a court.

User-fee goals (review days) are performance metrics. They are not a finding that the fee payor captured a particular decision. The preferential fact is the **budget share and the reauthorization bargain**, not a verdict on any NDA.

## Application Evidence

CRS R44750 (updated 2026): prescription-drug user fees were **7%** of PDUFA-program costs in FY1993 and **77%** in FY2025 (latest financial report cited there). Scope: PDUFA program costs, those fiscal years, not FDA's entire budget. HHS/ASPE on FY2022: user fees **66%** of the human-drugs program budget. Application fees for an NDA with clinical data are in the millions per FDA fee tables (FY2026 published rates).

CSA petitions and DEA Federal Register scheduling documents show "accepted medical use" as an FDA-and-consensus finding, not a street-clinic finding. Medical-board actions that cite association COVID-treatment guidance are the 2020s application: document as **which record was official**, not as a clinical-trial result.

## Framework notes

Relevant distinction rules from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md):

- **Rule 9 (Who defines harm).** The fee-and-association record defines medical harm and medical use.
- **Rule 18 (Authority: chosen or imposed?).** Official evidence is imposed as the condition of lawful practice.
- **Rule 20 (Manipulation vs Teaching).** Independent teaching that contradicts the record is routed as danger, not as argument.
- **Rule 22 (Legalism as Idol).** "Accepted medical use" and a user-fee clock become the law of the fact.
- **Rule 42 (Production: distributed or centralized).** Clinical observation is distributed; the mint of evidence is centralized.

Do not score this entry. The rules name the pattern; they do not replace the instruments or the evidence.
