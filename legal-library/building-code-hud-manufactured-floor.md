# Building code and HUD manufactured-housing floor

People with lumber, tools, and skill have long been able to raise a dwelling. Occupancy is another question. States adopt the International Building Code / International Residential Code. For manufactured homes, Congress passed the National Manufactured Housing Construction and Safety Standards Act of 1974, 42 U.S.C. §§ 5401 et seq.; HUD's construction standards (the HUD Code) took effect **June 15, 1976**. A HUD label is the federal franchise for a home built on a chassis in a factory. The analog owner-built house, tiny house, or pre-HUD mobile may be illegal to occupy even when it stands. This is technique enclosure in lumber: [pla-021](historical-technique-privilege.md) / [pla-003](manufacturer-repair-control.md) applied to the roof.

HUD standards **preempt conflicting construction standards**. They do **not** preempt local zoning of where the home may sit. That is the double wall with [pla-074](exclusionary-zoning-housing-wall.md).

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is that occupancy runs through a designated code or label. The parties who obtain it are code officials, HUD-certified manufacturers, and jurisdictions that keep unlabelled dwellings off the map. Owner-builders, tiny-house occupants, and pre-1976 mobile owners take tickets, condemnation, or exclusion from finance.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Occupy a dwelling you built or moved, on land you hold, without a HUD label or a full IRC inspection path. The closed analog is treating the analog person's technique as sufficient. Licensed contractor plus permit is the designated track, not a showing that the analog cannot build.

## Routing

Enabled: HUD-label manufacture; permitted site-built under the adopted code. Closed: occupy the analog unlabelled or owner-built dwelling as of right.

## Application Evidence

The 1974 Act and the 1976 HUD Code are the mechanism. HUD's Office of Manufactured Housing Programs inspects factories, authorizes certification labels, and pursues penalties. Scope: the statute, not a claim that every local code is a conspiracy.

Tiny-house and owner-builder prosecutions are jurisdiction-labeled illustrations (occupancy of a vehicle, a shed, or an unpermitted dwelling). Do not invent a national conviction count. Finance (FHA/GSE) that requires the HUD label or an IRC certificate is the credit overlay ([pla-008](secured-credit-foreclosure.md) adjacent).

Habitability codes on rental stock ([pla-077](habitability-paper-nonenforcement.md)) are the other side of the same inspector: strict on the analog occupant, discretionary on the landlord.
