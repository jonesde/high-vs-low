# Fiat issue and the first-receiver impulse

After domestic gold convertibility ended in 1933–34 and the remaining international gold window closed in August 1971, Federal Reserve notes and reserve balances are not a claim on a metal. Open-market operations, later large-scale asset purchases, and interest on reserves place **new purchasing power** first at a short list of counterparties. Street prices and wages move later. First receivers bid for land, equities, and goods at still-old prices. Later receivers get the diluted unit. A shrinking issue with the same delay would run the other way: the spigot's nearest parties would be first to lose nominal balances.

This page names the **impulse path**. It does not invent a percentage of "who inflation helps."

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a legally and operationally designated first receiver of new (or withdrawn) dollar balances: primary dealers in open-market operations, depository institutions that hold reserve accounts and receive interest on those balances, and whoever the Treasury pays when it spends newly borrowed dollars. Households whose income is a lagging wage or a cash balance take the delayed end.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

New money appearing first as a wage or a farm-gate payment; redeeming a note for gold at a fixed weight ([pla-028](gold-withdrawal-1933.md)); a shrinking monetary base that hits dealers and reserve-holders first. Those analogs are closed by fiat notes plus dealer-only OMOs plus IOR.

## Formal Legal Instruments

| ---------- | ------------------- | -------- |

| Primary dealers | NY Fed counterparties who must bid in OMOs and Treasury auctions — the operational first window | NY Fed, "Administration of Relationships with Primary Dealers" |
| Discount / advances | Member/depository access to the window | [12 U.S.C. §§ 343, 347](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/12/343) |
| Interest on reserves | The Board may pay interest on balances maintained at Reserve Banks (authorized 2006, used from 2008) | 12 U.S.C. § 461; Regulation D |
| End of gold convertibility | Aug. 15, 1971 suspension of dollar convertibility for foreign official holders | Nixon announcement / Proclamation 4074 lineage |
| Legal tender | First receivers can discharge debts at par immediately | [31 U.S.C. § 5103](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/31/5103); [pla-027](legal-tender-forced-acceptance.md) |

**First receiver (expansion):** the dealer who sells a Treasury or MBS to the Desk; the bank whose reserve account is credited; the Treasury vendor paid from the TGA after a debt sale. **Delayed end:** CPI-basket sellers and wage contracts that reset later; cash under the mattress.

**First loser (contraction):** the same dealer/bank/Treasury-counterparty complex, if the Desk sells or lets assets run off and reserve balances shrink — before wages have fallen.

## Routing

Enabled: FOMC direction to the NY Desk; dealer relationship policies; IOR as a floor paid only to institutions with reserve accounts. Closed: the public bidding directly at the window for new balances; gold redemption as the limit on issue.

IOR is not a public interest rate on cash. It is paid on balances at the Fed. That is a standing first-receiver income stream on the stock of reserves created by prior purchases.

## Application Evidence

**Structure.** NY Fed primary-dealer policy: dealers "participate consistently as counterparty to the New York Fed in its execution of open market operations" and "participate in all auctions of U.S. government debt." That sentence is the first-receiver designation. The public cannot.

**Sequence, not a fake percentage.** Large-scale asset purchases (2008–14, 2020–22) credit reserve accounts of banks through dealers. Asset prices that those institutions hold can move in the same window. Wage series and many consumer prices move over subsequent years. The library's claim is that **order**, which the facilities legally create, not a calibrated pass-through coefficient.

**1971.** Closing the gold window removes the remaining external brake on issue. Combined with § 355 purchases, the impulse is no longer capped by a metal ratio ([pla-026](federal-reserve-act-1913.md) original 40%).

Do not treat "inflation is always 2%" as the mechanism. The dual mandate (FRA § 2A) is a policy target. The first-receiver path is the legal machinery regardless of the target.

## Framework notes

Relevant distinction rules from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md):

- **Rule 1 (Pride vs Humility and Equality).** Access to new balances is a ranked slot, not a general condition.
- **Rule 15 (Response to loss: retake or expand).** Issue expands claims on real resources before the unit is marked down in wages.
- **Rule 22 (Legalism as Idol).** Par on the note plus dealer eligibility is the operating law of distribution.
- **Rule 36 (Treatment of the Least).** Lagging wages and cash are the delayed end.
- **Rule 38 (Consequences: natural or artificial).** Dilution is an artificial, path-dependent consequence of who is wired to the Desk.
- **Rule 42 (Production: distributed or centralized).** Street production is distributed; the impulse is centralized.
