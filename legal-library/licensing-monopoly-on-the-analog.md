# Licensing monopoly on the analog substance

A DEA-registered physician may prescribe oxycodone. An unregistered person with the same pills is a trafficker. Alcohol after 1933 is a state-licensed product; during Volstead it was a crime except on a permit ([pla-037](constitutional-purity-prohibition.md)). The preferential enabler is the **license that turns the analog from felony into professional service**. Health-complex guilds (medical boards, pharmacy boards, DEA registration) hold that switch. The Drug War is the unlicensed side of the same molecule.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a monopoly on lawful access. The parties who obtain it are registrants and the boards that can pull a number. Patients on the licensed track get a product (and, in the opioid-crisis years, sometimes a flood of it). Street users of the analog or of heroin get raids and mandatory minimums ([pla-039](mandatory-minimum-quantity-disparity.md)).

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Maintenance of an addict as "illegitimate practice" under Harrison ([pla-038](tax-schedule-as-crime.md)) vs. methadone/buprenorphine on a later licensed track. Off-label prescribing, which is lawful for physicians and a marketing crime for companies. The closed analog is a non-registrant possessing the same schedule II bottle.

**Other complex:** export licenses ([pla-016](technical-data-export-controls.md)); law licenses; security clearances. The MIC/intel analog is the clearance that makes the same technical data lawful in one mouth and a crime in another.

| PDMP / quota | Production and surveillance of the licensed flow | CSA quota provisions; state PDMPs |

## Routing

Enabled: administrative registration plus criminal law for everyone else. Closed: treating possession of a scheduled substance as a health or property problem without the guild gate.

Purdue and other opioid manufacturers operated on the licensed track, paid civil/criminal fines, and were not sentenced under 5-gram crack floors. That is [pla-010](white-collar-civil-routing.md) meeting this page.

## Application Evidence

21 U.S.C. § 822 is the gate. CDC and DEA opioid-quota and PDMP data (scope-label years) show a licensed flood and a later licensed drought — both administrative. Street fentanyl filled the gap as the unlicensed analog. The guild did not lose the monopoly; the molecule moved off the prescription pad.

Medical-board discipline for COVID speech or treatment (ivermectin, etc.) is a 2020s application: the board defines legitimate practice. Document as licensing-track compulsion, not as a clinical trial result.
