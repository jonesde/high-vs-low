# Faith-healing child exemptions

Child-neglect and homicide statutes ordinarily treat withholding medical care from a dying child as a crime. Many states, following a 1974 federal child-welfare funding cue, wrote **spiritual-treatment exceptions**: if the parent relied on prayer in lieu of medicine, the omission is not neglect. Analog parents — no church, no exemption language — are prosecuted for the same death. Oregon narrowed its exemption after Followers of Christ cases; Idaho and other states retained broader versions (date the code). Underage marriage in sects is the related delay: *Reynolds* already said polygamy is not Free Exercise, yet prosecutions lagged for decades.

*Wisconsin v. Yoder* (1972) is the educational overlay: Amish parents may pull children from school after eighth grade. Distinguish community conscience from using *Yoder* as a general shield against child-protection. This page is the **exemption from the ordinary crime**, not a verdict on Amish schooling.

This page does not decide whether prayer heals. It names who is exempt when a child dies.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is a religious defense or a charging decline. The parties who obtain it are parents and groups whose tradition is written into the code, and the institutions that taught the withholding. The child takes the residual. Analog families take the criminal track.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

A parent who withholds insulin or antibiotics without a statutory spiritual exception. A physician who withholds care and faces licensing and homicide exposure. The closed analog is treating the child's body as the same legal object regardless of the parent's church.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** *Reynolds* / *Smith* close unfavored **adult** practice. Spiritual-treatment exemptions open a defense for **favored** withholding that kills a child. Same "free exercise" vocabulary.

**Other complex:** CSA analog for the unlicensed molecule ([pla-038](tax-schedule-as-crime.md)); licensed guild for the same molecule ([pla-044](licensing-monopoly-on-the-analog.md)). Here the "license" is the statutory religious defense.

Oregon's post-Followers-of-Christ amendments (including 2011 homicide-defense limits) are evidence that the exemption was the operating law **until** a cluster of deaths made repeal politically possible. Idaho's remaining exemptions are the unreformed analog. Scope-label by state and year.

## Routing

Enabled: statutory defense; prosecutor decline; church teaching that medicine is faithlessness. Closed: ordinary manslaughter/neglect charge for the same omission.

FLDS/Jeffs: Texas conviction (2011) for child sexual assault after years of open underage "marriage." The delay, not the eventual conviction, is the preferential routing. Label alleged historical practice; the conviction is a court judgment.

## Application Evidence

Followers of Christ prosecutions in Oregon (2008–2011 cluster and later) are the named application that forced statutory change. Christian Science exemptions appear in multiple state codes historically; several states repealed after documented child deaths. Children's Healthcare Is a Legal Duty (CHILD) and state health-department reviews compile case lists — cite those compilations by year rather than inventing a national death total.

*Yoder* remains good law for its facts. Using it to block medical or abuse inquiries is a different routing, and should be labeled as such.
