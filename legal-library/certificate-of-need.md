# Certificate of need

A certificate of need (CON) is a state permission slip to open or expand a health facility, add beds, or buy a designated machine. New York's Metcalf-McCloskey Act (1964) is an origin illustration. The National Health Planning and Resources Development Act of 1974, Pub. L. 93-641, induced states to adopt CON by tying federal health-planning money to a program. Congress repealed the federal mandate in 1986; **most states kept some CON**. Incumbents contest applications. The analog is an equipped clinic that opens as of right. License-as-wall cousin of [pla-044](licensing-monopoly-on-the-analog.md); incumbent-discretion cousin of [pla-009](zoning-incumbent-protection.md).

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is that new capacity is a contested administrative franchise. The parties who obtain it are incumbent hospitals and the boards that hear them. Equipped analog providers take the residual: delay, denial, or a different service line that is not on the CON list.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

An equipped outpatient clinic, imaging device, or bed tower that opens without asking incumbents. The closed analog is treating health facilities like other buildings that pass a building code ([pla-075](building-code-hud-manufactured-floor.md) adjacent) without a competitor veto.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** CON is sold as cost control. Hospital associations were often the constituency that kept it after 1986. The federal experiment was repealed; the local franchise remained.

## Routing

Enabled: administrative denial or delay; incumbent participation in the hearing. Closed: as-of-right opening of an equipped clinic.

## Application Evidence

NHPRDA 1974 and the 1986 repeal are the federal hinge. Mercatus (2020/2021): **35 states and D.C.** required a CON for at least one service as of January 2020. Later 50-state scans (NASHP and others) put retained programs in the **mid-to-high 30s** depending on whether nursing-home-only or single-service programs count. Scope-label the scanner and year. Do not invent a national denial rate.

Incumbent opposition at CON hearings is the application: the competitor is a party. That is [pla-009](zoning-incumbent-protection.md) in a health-facility register.
