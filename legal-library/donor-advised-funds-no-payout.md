# Donor-advised funds with no payout duty

A donor contributes to a donor-advised fund at a sponsoring organization (Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, a community foundation). § 170 allows the deduction **that year**. § 170(f)(18) requires the sponsor's acknowledgment that the sponsor has **exclusive legal control**. The donor retains advisory privileges. Unlike private foundations, DAFs have **no** § 4942 5% minimum distribution. §§ 4966–4967 (Pension Protection Act 2006) tax "taxable distributions" and excess-benefit deals; they do not impose an annual grant duty. Analog: money to a working kitchen this year.

Controllers: commercial DAF sponsors and community-foundation DAF programs. The operating charity is downstream, if the advisor ever recommends a grant. Anonymity plus delayed grant is opacity next to church no-990 ([pla-052](church-tax-opacity.md)) — public charities file 990s; the **account-level** wait is still dark.

This page does not decide whether DAFs "increase giving." It names when the deduction attaches and who need not pay out.

## Outcome / Goal

The practical result is deducted capital at a sponsor. The parties who obtain it are donors (immediate deduction, continued advice) and sponsors (AUM). Operating charities take grants if and when advised. Analog recipients take nothing while the account sits.

## Contrast / Parallel Track

Private-foundation 5% ([pla-067](private-foundation-perpetual-control.md)). A complete gift to an operating 501(c)(3) that spends. The closed analog is no deduction until a grant reaches an operating charity, or a DAF payout floor matching § 4942.

**Hypocrisy (two tracks):** 1969 imposed 5% on family foundations after abuse hearings. 2006 defined DAFs and still omitted the floor. Commercial sponsors then became some of the largest "charities" by receipts.

## Routing

Enabled: complete deduction at contribution; advisory privileges; no annual payout. Closed: deduction timed to operating-charity receipt; analog 5% duty.

## Application Evidence

DAF Research Collaborative *Annual DAF Report 2025* (FY2024): assets **$327.87 billion**; contributions **$90.57 billion**; grants **$64.60 billion**; aggregate payout rate **25.2%** (their methodology). NPT *2024 DAF Report* (FY2023): assets **$251.52 billion**; grants **$54.77 billion**; payout **23.9%**. Scope: those reports, those fiscal years, those payout formulas (grants / prior-year or start-of-year assets). Aggregate rates can exceed 20% while **inactive accounts** and commercial AUM still defer. Do not invent an inactivity percentage. The preferential fact remains: **no statute requires the grant**.
